# Assessment Protocol — Triangulation Without a Scanner

How to move from presentation to biotype when fMRI isn't available. The method is
**triangulation**: no single data point is decisive; converge three independent
sources.

## The three legs of triangulation

1. **Symptom clustering** — where does the chief complaint live?
   - *In the head* (looping thoughts) → Default Mode
   - *In the body* (palpitations, knots) → Salience
   - *In the mood* (intense misery) → Negative Affect
   - *In the flatness* (numb, no drive) → Positive Affect
   - *In the focus* (drifting, lethargic) → Attention
   - *In the executive* (fog, can't decide, impulsive) → Cognitive Control
2. **Self-report scales** — BRISC for rapid triage (Negativity Bias vs
   Resilience), plus symptom-specific scales (RRS, GAD-7/DASS-21, CTQ, SHAPS/DARS).
3. **Computerized cognitive proxies** — WebNeuro/IntegNeuro tasks whose Z-scores
   stand in for the fMRI biotype signatures (Go/NoGo, Emotion ID, CPT/Oddball,
   Digit Span).

Agreement across ≥2 legs raises confidence; disagreement is a prompt to use the
tie-breakers below rather than to force a single label.

## Workflow

1. **Intake.** Take a history, or run the 24-item questionnaire
   (`questionnaire.md`).
2. **Score / cluster.** Compute the six normalized scores; note the primary
   (highest) and any circuit ≥ 40%.
3. **Differentiate.** Resolve overlaps with the tie-breakers.
4. **Corroborate.** Where available, add BRISC / symptom scales and any
   WebNeuro proxy results.
5. **Treatment considerations.** Read `treatment-matrix.md`; flag SSRI-underperformer
   biotypes.
6. **Frame + safeguard.** Present as clinician-facing screening; escalate acute
   risk before anything else.

## Differential tie-breakers (the hard pairs)

**Default Mode vs Attention — both look "distracted."**
- Default Mode: pulled *inward* by their own thoughts; can focus if the internal
  loop quiets; **initial insomnia** from racing mind.
- Attention: pulled *outward* / simply drifts; lethargic and "checked out";
  **errors of omission** on CPT; poor Digit Span.

**Negative Affect vs Positive Affect — both are "depressed."**
- Negative Affect: **intense** misery, guilt, fear; catastrophizing; reacts *fast*
  to threat. Good SSRI responder.
- Positive Affect: **numbness/emptiness**, anhedonia, apathy; "I don't care";
  no reward speed-up. SSRI-resistant → dopaminergic.

**Salience vs Negative Affect — both "anxious."**
- Salience: anxiety is **somatic/visceral**, avoidance, startle; **slowed/frozen**
  reaction to threat faces.
- Negative Affect: anxiety is **cognitive/valenced** (threat appraisal, misery);
  **fast/vigilant** reaction to threat faces.

**Attention vs Cognitive Control — both "can't think straight."**
- Attention: can't **sustain** focus; **omission** errors (misses targets).
- Cognitive Control: can't **inhibit**; **commission** errors (Go/NoGo), high
  Stroop interference, decision paralysis, impulsivity.

## Confidence and reporting

- **High confidence:** one clearly dominant circuit + concordant scale/proxy.
- **Mixed profile:** report primary + secondary circuits; treat the highest-yield
  actionable finding first (e.g. flag SSRI-underperformer biotypes early).
- Always state: screening only, assumes honest self-report, not a diagnosis,
  route to a licensed clinician.

## Red-flag override

If the presentation includes **suicidal ideation, self-harm, or danger to self or
others**, stop biotype scoring and direct the person to emergency/crisis services
first. Biotyping is never a substitute for risk assessment.
