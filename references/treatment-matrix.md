# Bedside Biotype Matrix — Treatment Reference

One-page "triangulation" table from *"Brain Circuits: Clinical Identification for
Psychiatrists."* Combine (1) the primary complaint, (2) a behavioral sign, (3) a
cognitive proxy, and (4) a self-report scale to land a biotype, then read across
to preferred pharmacology (Rx) and psychotherapy (Tx).

| Biotype | Primary complaint | Key behavioral sign | Proxy cognitive test (WebNeuro/IntegNeuro) | Self-report indicator | Preferred **Rx** | Preferred **Tx** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Default Mode** | "I can't shut my mind off" (rumination) | Distracted, internal focus | Emotion ID: **slowed** reaction to **sad** faces | BRISC high Negativity Bias + RRS high Rumination | SSRI / **Vortioxetine** | CBT (rumination-focused), Behavioral Activation |
| **Salience** | "I feel anxiety in my body" (physical) | Avoidance, startle, overwhelm | Emotion ID: **slowed** reaction to threat (frozen) | GAD-7 high somatic items; BRISC high Negativity Bias | SSRI **+ beta-blocker** | Exposure therapy (interoceptive), Mindfulness |
| **Negative Affect** | "I feel threatened / miserable" (mood) | Catastrophizing, irritability | Emotion ID: **fast** reaction to threat (vigilance) or bias (neutral = sad) | CTQ trauma history; BRISC high Negativity Bias | **SSRI** (escitalopram) | Cognitive restructuring, Trauma therapy |
| **Positive Affect** | "I feel numb / empty" (anhedonia) | Apathy, low motor output | Reward tasks: **no** reaction-time speed-up for reward | SHAPS high; DARS low motivation | **DA agent** (pramipexole, bupropion) | Behavioral Activation (savoring / PAT) |
| **Attention** | "I can't focus" (drifting) | Errors of omission, zoning out | CPT / Oddball: errors of **omission** (missed targets) | Digit Span poor | **SNRI** (venlafaxine) | Cognitive remediation (**Rx first**) |
| **Cognitive Control** | "Brain fog / can't decide" | Impulsivity, rigidity | Go/NoGo: errors of **commission**; Stroop: high interference | BRISC low Resilience; poor executive history | **SNRI**, **TMS** | Problem-Solving Therapy (PST) |

## Prescribing flags (the high-value clinical shortcuts)

- **SSRIs underperform** in **Positive Affect** (serotonin blunts striatal
  dopamine → worse anhedonia) and **Cognitive Control** (~38% vs ~47% remission).
  These are the biotypes to route *away* from default first-line SSRIs.
- **Attention** biotype: **medication before psychotherapy** — restore attention
  first or the therapy "dose" won't land.
- **Salience** biotype: add an autonomic dampener (beta-blocker / alpha-2 agonist)
  to break the body↔insula loop.
- **Negative Affect** biotype: the classic **good SSRI responder** (serotonin
  downregulates amygdala firing).
- **Cognitive Control** biotype: **prime TMS candidate** (DLPFC target).

## Non-imaging assessment toolbox

- **BRISC** (Brief Risk-resilience Index for Screening, 15-item): normalized
  Z-scores for **Negativity Bias**, **Emotional Resilience**, **Social Skills**.
  Rapid triage — high Negativity Bias → Negative Affect / Salience / Default Mode;
  low Resilience → Cognitive Control / Attention.
- **WebNeuro / IntegNeuro** (~30 min online battery, the iSPOT-D cognitive tests):
  Z-scores on Go/NoGo, Emotion ID, CPT/Oddball, Digit Span — the behavioral
  proxies for the fMRI biotypes. Single most effective clinic upgrade toward
  precision diagnosis. (CANTAB is an alternative battery.)
- **Symptom-specific scales:** RRS (rumination), GAD-7 / DASS-21 (somatic
  anxiety), CTQ (trauma), SHAPS / DARS (anhedonia).

> ⚠️ Treatment considerations are **educational decision-support**, not a
> prescription. All medication choices require a licensed prescriber weighing
> full history, contraindications, and interactions.
